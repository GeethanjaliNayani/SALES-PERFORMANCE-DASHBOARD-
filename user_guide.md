****User Guide – Sales Performance Analytics Dashboard****

This dashboard provides sales performance insights across markets, products, and customers.

**KPIs**
1. Revenue (₹986.57M) → Total sales revenue.
2. Sales Quantity (2M) → Total quantity sold.

**Visuals**
1. Revenue by Market → Bar chart ranking markets by revenue.
2. Sales Qty by Customer → Bar chart showing top customers by quantity purchased.
3. Revenue Trend → Line chart tracking monthly revenue over time.
4. Top 5 Customers by Revenue → Highest revenue-generating customers.
5. Top 5 Products by Revenue → Best-selling products by revenue share.

**Filters**
1. Year filter → Switch between 2017–2020.
2. Month filter → Analyze monthly performance trends.
